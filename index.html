SHADOWED ECHOES

A Victorian Horror Experience
You awaken on the cold stone floor of Ashwood Manor, your memory a shredded ruin. Rain hammers the shuttered windows. The grandfather clock in the hall has stopped at 3:13 AM. Somewhere deep in the house, something exhales.

The Ashwood family vanished seventeen years ago. The locals say the house consumed them. They say it is still hungry. They say something still walks its halls — something that was once a man, before grief and obsession hollowed it out and filled the void with darkness.

Find the golden key. Find the truth. Escape before dawn — or join the Ashwoods forever.
